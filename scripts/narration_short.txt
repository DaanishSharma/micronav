## card | Every sensor in your phone lies | GPS jumps by metres  ·  the gyro drifts 25° a minute | the accelerometer is 119 m off within a minute
Every sensor in your phone lies. GPS jumps around, the gyroscope drifts, and the accelerometer is a hundred metres off within a minute. So I learned sensor fusion from zero, and built micronav: an open-source positioning engine.

## drive
Here's a real drive, recorded on one iPhone with phyphox. Orange is raw GPS. Blue is micronav's fifteen-state Kalman filter: the motion sensors predict a hundred times a second, GPS corrects once a second, and it even works out how the phone sits in the car.

## outage
Now hide GPS for thirty seconds out of every ninety. A GPS-only filter misses by twenty-nine metres. micronav misses by nine, because cars don't slide sideways.

## end
